The Vortex Standard

Our Athlete Commitment Charter

Athletic excellence requires dedication. Athletes sign our charter upon entry.

  • Relentless Effort on Every Rep

    We demand 100% intentionality in warmups, shooting drills, and game simulations regardless of fatigue.

  • Zero-Excuse Teammate Accountability

    Athletes communicate constructively, rotate proactively on defense, and hold peers to high behavioral standards.

  • Academic & NCAA Eligibility Focus

    Collegiate prospect athletes must maintain minimum 3.0 GPA benchmarks alongside their on-court training schedule.

  • Psychological Poise Under Duress

    We train players to thrive in hostile atmospheres, execute under referee whistles, and maintain mental fortitude.
